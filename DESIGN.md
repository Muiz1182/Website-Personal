# DESIGN.md: MMA PRODUCTION Visual Identity Direction

## 1. Identity & Character
- **Brand**: MMA PRODUCTION (Directed by Mochammad Muiz Afdloly).
- **Core Domain**: High-impact cinematography, precision post-production & video editing, cutting-edge motion graphics.
- **Personality**: Visionary, precise, cinematic, editorial, and commanding. Not a generic tech/SaaS website, but a film & creative studio portfolio that breathes moving images and cinematic craft.

## 2. Visual Language: Cinematic Editorial
- **Philosophy**: Every frame counts. Content leads the layout. Large typographic rhythm inspired by high-end film title cards and editorial journals (A24, Criterion, Nowness style).
- **Palette**:
  - Base Dark: `#070707` (True Obsidian Black)
  - Surface Dark: `#0E0E0F` (Deep Charcoal Slate)
  - Surface Border: `#1F1F24` (Subtle Cinematic Edge)
  - Primary Text: `#F5F5F7` (Pristine Titanium White)
  - Muted Text: `#A1A1AA` (Subtle Silver Gray, WCAG AA compliant >= 4.5:1)
  - Deliberate Accent: `#FF5722` (Cinematic Vermillion / Film Roll Ember) — used exclusively for active focus and key moments, avoiding the generic blue/purple AI slop.
- **Typography**:
  - Display / Headings: `Plus Jakarta Sans` & `Syne` / clean geometric uppercase with tight tracking for title cards.
  - Body: `Inter` for crisp, effortless readability.
- **Texture**: Clean matte dark surfaces with fine film-grade hairline borders, no generic floating shadows, no rainbow mesh gradients, no glowing orbs.

## 3. Dials
- **ENERGY**: 2 (Balanced, confident, atmospheric without excessive noise)
- **RHYTHM**: 3 (Varied compositions: full-screen cinematic hero, asymmetric work showcase, horizontal film reel ticker, immersive director profile, clean project inquiry sheet)
- **MOTION**: 2 (Deliberate transitions, silky hover previews on projects, zero endless pulsing loops or fidgeting decorations)

## 4. Content & Honesty
- Real portfolio assets for Mochammad Muiz Afdloly with functional preview states.
- Clean working contact form with real feedback and instant communication paths (WhatsApp & Direct Email).
- No fake ratings (e.g. "4.9/5 stars" with fake names deleted or replaced with actual project credits).
- No fake skill bars ("Video Editing 95%"). Replaced by genuine craft deliverables and workflow capabilities.
